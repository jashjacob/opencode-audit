# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone. Be considerate, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project welcoming.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/jashjacob).
